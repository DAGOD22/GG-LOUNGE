Paper.io 2 is an io game where you claim territory by drawing loops across the map and filling them in with your color. Venture out, encircle as much space as possible, and cut off rivals before they cut off you. The further you roam from your safe zone, the more exposed your tail becomes — and one snip from an opponent sends you back to the start. Bold moves win big, but greed gets you eliminated. How much of the map can you paint yours?

How to play Paper.io 2?

Use WASD, the arrow keys or the joystick to move.
